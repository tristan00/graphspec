**The contract: schema, time, tasks, kinds of target, edges beyond pairs, held graphs, tables and bank version 13**

A source holds one or more graphs. What the graphs hold is stated once, by a schema; the tables of the source and the bank built from them are checked against it. Nothing is read from the names or the types of the tables beyond what the schema states.

**Schema**

The schema is one object, `graphspec.Schema`, built from a plain mapping and written back to one by `mapping()`. It has no defaults: a field that is absent stops the work with an error naming it. `graphspec.inspect(path)` and the verb `graphspec inspect PATH` print it for a task file or for a bank; for a bank they add the relations found. Widths are not printed: they depend on the encoders fitted with the views of a statement.

| Field | Holds |
|---|---|
| `version` | The version of the schema, 6. |
| `time` | `model`, one of `none`, `order`, `clock`, `clocks`; `units`, the names of the units in which the graphs of the source may count time, stated when the model has a clock and empty when it has none; `ties`, the name of the rule that orders facts with the same time, or `null`. |
| `observers` | `true` when facts and tasks may name observers, `false` when none does. |
| `columns` | Every column by name, each with its `type` and its `encoder`. A column is one thing wherever it is used: a name listed by two kinds is the same column, fitted once. |
| `graph` | `columns`: the attributes of the graph itself. |
| `nodes` | Per kind of node, `columns`, the columns of that kind, and `holds`, `null` or the statement of the graphs its nodes hold. Kinds keep the order stated. |
| `sets` | Per kind of set edge: `kinds`, the kinds of node its members may have; `roles`, the names of the roles a member may take; `members`, the columns of values every member carries; `columns`, the columns of the set edge itself. An empty mapping states none. |
| `edges` | Per edge table: `endpoints`, the pairs of kinds it joins, each a kind of node or a kind of set edge; `directed`, true or false; `columns`, the values its edges carry; `relation`, either `"declared"`, which makes the table one relation of its own name, or `{"column": NAME}`, which takes the relation of every row from that column; `about`, `null` when both ends of every pair are kinds of node, and otherwise the name of the rule for a time at which the set edge an edge is about does not hold. |
| `tasks` | `key`, the name of the column that identifies a task; `grouping`, the name of a column that groups tasks, or `null` when none is stated; `signals`, the width of the signal vectors, 0 for none; `references`, every reference by name; `kinds`, every kind of task by name. |

Kinds of node, kinds of set edge and edge tables have names distinct from one another. A column that the schema declares and that no kind of node or of set edge, no edge table, no graph held by a node, no kind of task and not the graph uses stops the schema with an error naming it. A column of the graph that is also a column of a kind of task stops the schema with an error naming it, since a task holds the columns of its graph among its own encoded values.

A reference states its `sort` and `kinds`, the kinds its table starts from, each a kind of node or a kind of set edge; a sort that stores nothing states no kinds. A member of a kind of set edge names the set edge by its key. A kind of task states:

| Field of a kind of task | Holds |
|---|---|
| `references` | The names of its references. No family reads their order: a family reads the references of a task as a set keyed by name (see What a family reads). |
| `columns` | Its features. |
| `target` | `null`, or `kind`, `series`, `about`, `utility` and the parameters its kind of target states, such as `relation` for a link: `kind`, a kind of target registered in `graphspec.TARGETS` (see Kinds of target); `series`, the series its targets belong to, in the form the kind reads, the name of the one series for `number` and the list of the series its points may name for `path`; `about`, the name of the one of its references the target is about, or `null` when it is about none of them, such as a target about the graph; `utility`, the value of an outcome (see Utilities). `about` and `utility` have no default. Kinds of task of one schema may state targets of different kinds, and one bank holds them together. A series belongs to targets of one kind of target: a series named by two kinds of target stops the schema naming it. |
| `weight` | `true` when every task of the kind carries a weight above zero and finite at float32 precision, `false` when none does. A weight that is not stops the build with an error naming the task, its kind and its graph. |
| `absent` | What a reference to a node not observed at the time of the task resolves to: `stop`, which stops the build and every derivation with an error naming the reference, the task and the graph, or `keep`, which keeps the reference and gives the model nothing to read for that node. It does not apply to a kind whose target is a `link` for the reference its link starts from and for the node it links to: both are observed at the time of the task, or the build and every view stop with an error naming the reference or the target, the task, its kind, the graph and the node. After the operations of a pipeline, the reference a link starts from follows `absent` as any reference does, and a task whose link starts from or names a node the variant does not hold at its step is left out (see Kinds of target). |
| `event` | `null`, or two of its references of the sort `node`: a task of the kind is then seen by later tasks as an event between the two, their nodes are the pair of the task, and its kind states `absent` as `stop`. |
| `matures` | `null`, or `reference` and `column`: the target of a task of the kind, once known, is read as the values of columns of the node the reference names. The reference is of the sort `node`; the target is of a kind of target that states the type of the columns it matures into (see Kinds of target); `column` is, as that kind reads it, one column, or a mapping from every class of the target to a column, each of that type and a column of every kind of node the reference may name. A kind of target that states no type, such as `path`, does not mature, and a kind of task that states what matures of it stops the schema naming it. |

Types of column are registered by name in `graphspec.TYPES`, encoders in `graphspec.ENCODERS`, encoders of held graphs in `graphspec.HELD`, sorts of reference in `graphspec.SORTS`, kinds of target in `graphspec.TARGETS`, utilities in `graphspec.UTILITIES`, rules for edges about edges in `graphspec.ABOUT`, forms of storage of set edges in `graphspec.STORAGE`; each accepts registration from outside the library, and a name registered twice stops with an error. Every entry of a registry that derivation, views or packing read states its version, a whole number from 1 raised whenever its code changes, as the attribute `version` of itself or of its own class, never one it inherits: types of column, encoders, encoders of held graphs, sorts of reference, kinds of target, rules for ties, choices among several facts, rules for edges about edges, forms of storage, choices of steps, rules of what a split may see, rules of negative sampling, representations and operations. `graphspec.schema.references.Sort`, `graphspec.operations.sets.Form`, `graphspec.operations.negatives.Rule`, `graphspec.Representation` and `graphspec.Operation` take it as an argument; a function registered as an entry carries it as an attribute set after its definition. An entry that states none stops its registration naming the registry and the entry. The name and version of every entry the views of a statement read enter the digest of their folder, and those of every entry a variant reads its key (see The key of a variant). Shorthand forms, utilities, heads, losses and measures, which no cache reads, state none. The shipped entries are at version 1, and `tests/test_standing.py` holds the version and a digest of the code of each, failing when the code changes and the version does not. Every entry registered from outside the library, in any registry, is passed to every process the library starts (the workers of a build, of a derivation and of packing, the pool that draws candidates, and the worker of a trial) and registered there before use, so that a worker holds in every registry exactly the entries the process that started it holds at that time: an entry the worker holds under the name of another, as one a module registers when the worker imports it, is replaced by the caller's, one the caller does not hold is removed, and an entry whose version in the worker, read from the code the worker imports, differs from the caller's stops the worker naming its registry, its name and both versions; an object defined at the top level of a module is passed by reference, and its module must be importable in the worker. An entry that cannot be passed stops the call that starts the workers with an error naming its registry and its name, and an error while a worker starts reaches the caller with its message.

| Type | Stored in a source as | Kept in the bank as |
|---|---|---|
| `number` | `FLOAT` | the value |
| `flag` | `BOOL` | 0 or 1 |
| `category` | `STRING` | the position of the value in the dictionary of the column, which holds every value stated and every class of a target that matures into the column |

A missing value is a null in the source and is kept as missing.

| Encoder | Reads | Gives | Fitted state | A value it did not see when fitted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `value` | number, flag | the value, 0 where missing; a flag of presence | none | nothing is fitted, every value is read as it is |
| `logarithm` | number | sign times log of one plus the size; a flag of presence | none | nothing is fitted, every value is read as it is |
| `standard` | number | the value less the mean over the deviation, 0 where missing; a flag of presence | mean and deviation, from at least two differing values | a value outside the range fitted on is standardised as any other, and stops only where it passes the limit of 16 bits below |
| `onehot` | category | one place per value kept, one more shared by every other value; a flag of presence | the values kept, by name, possibly none | stated by its registration: `shared` gives it the shared place, `stop` stops naming it; the shipped `onehot` is `shared` |

An encoder is registered with `fit(codes, dictionary, column)`, which gives its state, `widths(state)`, the widths of its value part and of its flag, and `encode(codes, state, dictionary)`; `dictionary` is the dictionary of a column of categories and `None` for any other. What an encoder does with a value it did not see when fitted is part of its registration and stated with it, never a silent substitute.

The bank keeps the values as stated and the dictionaries only. Every fitted state of an encoder is fitted when the views of a statement are built (see Views), from exactly what the views of the splits trained on show, and is stored with those views. The rows fitted on are, in every view whose targets include a target of a split trained on, and up to and including the last step at which such a target's task sits: the values of the nodes and set edges it shows, once per step shown, the matured targets among them; the values of the facts about edges it shows, once per step, and of the absent facts it shows; the columns of its tasks of the splits trained on and of its tasks that name no split; the attributes of the graph; and the held nodes of the graphs held by the nodes it shows. What a split's rules keep from it, the entities named only by tasks of the splits scored on, the columns and the targets of the tasks of other splits, and what the clock hides are therefore never fitted on. The views of a statement show the attributes of the graph, the columns of the kinds of node, of set edge and of task, the columns of the edge tables, the columns of held nodes, and the member columns of the kinds of set edge whose form of storage shows the values of members; they never show the columns of held edges. A column that no view of the statement shows is not encoded: its state is null, it has no width, and nothing stops for it. `standard` stops, naming the column, where a column shown holds fewer than two differing values in those rows; `onehot` keeps no value where they hold none.

The width of a kind is the sum of the widths its columns encode to, and for a kind that holds graphs the width of its encoding of held graphs. Values of nodes, of set edges and of tasks are encoded into one table whose columns are the value parts of every column of the kinds of node, then of the kinds of set edge, then of the kinds of task, then of the graph, in the order stated, followed by their flags, followed by one block per kind that holds graphs, in the order of the kinds; a row holds zeros in the columns and blocks its kind does not have. The row of a task holds the attributes of its graph in the columns of the graph, so that every family reads them as part of the task; no other row holds them. The first linear layer of a model therefore holds one block of weights per column and per encoding of held graphs, and reads each kind through its own blocks. Values on edges are kept as stated and encoded at 32 bits when a batch is packed.

The encoded table of nodes, set edges and tasks, which is what models read, is stored at 16 bits in every view. It holds magnitudes up to 65504 with about three significant digits; a magnitude below about 6e-5 keeps fewer digits, and one below about 6e-8 becomes 0. An encoded value beyond 65504, or not finite, stops the building of the views that meet it with an error naming the node or the task, its kind, its view, the column, the value as stated and the encoder. The values as stated are kept at 32 bits. An infinite value stated for a node, a set edge or a task stops the build naming the graph and the column.

**Edges joining sets**

A kind of set edge is a kind of its own. A set edge has a key, like a node; its facts, like the facts about a node, carry its interval, the time it was known and replaced, the observers who see it and the values of its own columns, and pass through the one guard of visibility. Its members are fixed: one row per member, naming the node, its role and the values of the member columns of the kind. A set edge has at least one member; a node is a member of one set edge at most once in one role; a member carries a value in every member column. The members of a set edge are observed at every time the set edge holds, as the ends of an edge are.

An edge table whose endpoints name a kind of set edge holds edges about set edges: an edge from or to a set edge, with an interval, known time and observers of its own. An edge that must be the end of another edge is stated as a set edge, of two members with roles when it joins two nodes. Its rule, named by `about`, says what becomes of an edge about a set edge at a time when that set edge is not shown to the task looking; rules are registered in `graphspec.ABOUT`, each with `kept(lacking, named)`, which gives for every such row whether it is kept.

| Rule | At a time when the set edge it is about is not shown |
|---|---|
| `stop` | The edge stops the build and every view with an error naming the edge, its ends, the graph and the time. |
| `lapse` | The edge is not shown at that time; it is shown at the other times it holds. |

The engine reads nodes and edges between two nodes. What a set edge becomes is chosen by the settings of the pipeline, through a setting `sets/<kind>/storage` for every kind of set edge. The setting has no default: a pipeline that does not state it stops naming it. Forms are registered in `graphspec.STORAGE` as `graphspec.operations.sets.Form(function, nodes, members, version)`: a function from a graph of facts, the registry and the kind to a graph of facts, whether it makes each set edge a node, whether it shows the values of the members, and its version; `node` shows them and `pairs` does not, so under `pairs` a member column no other use shows is not encoded. A form that makes no node is not offered for a kind that an edge table is about, that a reference of a kind of task stating `absent` as `stop` names, or that the reference a target is about names.

| Form | A set edge becomes | Its relations |
|---|---|---|
| `node` | A node of its kind, with its facts as the values of the node, and for every fact and every member an edge from the node to the member, of the relation of the member's role, carrying the values of the member, with the interval, known time and observers of the fact | `<kind>.<role>`, one per role, from the kind of set edge to each kind of its members, directed, with the member columns |
| `pairs` | For every fact, one edge between every two distinct members, carrying the values of the set edge's own columns, with the interval, known time and observers of the fact; the set edge is no node, and roles and member values are not read | `<kind>`, between the kinds of its members, without direction, with the columns of the set edge |

These relations are relations of the registry like those stated: each has a code, is kept or dropped by `relations/<name>/keep`, filtered by the parameters of its edges and projected. A relation stated or taken whose name is one of them stops the build. The two form functions are `graphspec.operations.sets.node` and `graphspec.operations.sets.pairs`: `node` adds facts; `pairs` adds facts and removes the observations of the set edges, so it may remove a referenced set edge. A reference to a set edge under a form that makes no node resolves as a node not observed, by the kind's statement of `absent`.

**Nodes that hold graphs**

A kind of node states under `holds` the schema of the graphs its nodes hold: `nodes`, per held kind, `columns`; `edges`, per held relation, `endpoints` (pairs of held kinds), `directed` and `columns`; `encoder`, the name of the encoder of held graphs. The columns are columns of the schema, fitted as any other. A node holds one graph, stated once, possibly empty; a held node has a key unique within the graph it belongs to; a held edge joins two held nodes of one graph, of kinds its relation joins in either order when it has no direction. A held node states only the columns of its held kind, and a held edge only those of its relation.

The encoder turns every held graph of the kind into one vector, which is the block of the kind in the encoded values of every fact about the node. Encoders of held graphs are registered in `graphspec.HELD`, each with `width(encoding, held)` and `encode(encoding, held, graphs)`, `graphs` holding `owners`, the number of nodes of the kind, and for every held node `owner` and `values`, and for every held edge `source` and `target`.

| Encoder | Gives, for a held graph | Width |
|---|---|---|
| `pooled` | The sum over its held nodes of their encoded columns, then the number of its held nodes | the width of its held columns plus one |
| `propagated` | The sum over its held nodes of their encoded columns, of the mean of the encoded columns of their neighbours over held edges in either direction (zero for a node without one), then the number of its held nodes | twice the width of its held columns plus one |

Neither shipped encoder reads the values of held edges, and no encoder of held graphs is given them; they are stored and read back, and their columns are not encoded.

**Operations that count**

Operations that count degree, core numbers, components or paths meet set edges and edges about edges in the form the pipeline chose. What an operation requires of the schema is a tuple of counts, `graphspec.operations.operation.Count(name, counts, active, sets, about)`: what it counts, a function of the registry and the settings of the operation that says when a pipeline makes it act, and its treatment of the edges joining sets and of the edges about edges: `stored`, it counts the form as ordinary nodes and edges; or `refuses`, it stops. A count that states no treatment of either stops where it is made, naming the count. An operation called on a graph whose schema states a kind or table that one of its acting counts refuses stops before it runs, with an error naming the count, the operation and the kind or table.

| Count | Counts | Set edges | Edges about edges |
|---|---|---|---|
| `nodes/degree`, `nodes/total_degree` | degree | stored: under `node` a member counts one edge per set edge it is in and a set edge's node counts its members and the edges about it; under `pairs` a member of a set edge of k members counts k − 1 | stored: one edge at the set edge's node and one at the other end |
| `nodes/core` | core numbers | stored, as for degree | stored |
| `derive/hops` | paths | stored: under `node` two members of one set edge are two hops apart, under `pairs` one | stored: through the set edge's node |
| `neighbourhood/hops` | paths | stored, as for `derive/hops` | stored: through the set edge's node |
| `derive/projection` | paths of two edges | stored: projecting a relation of a role over its members gives the members that share a set edge | stored |
| `derive/diffusion` | components and paths | stored | stored |
| `derive/rewiring` | components and degree | stored | stored |
| `product/path` | paths | stored: a path through the relations of a role walks through the set edge's node | stored |
| `negatives/degree` | degree | stored: a member's degree in the relations of the roles is the number of sets it is a member of | stored |
| `negatives/hops` | paths | stored, as for `derive/hops` | stored |
| `edges/cap` | degree | stored: the cap of a relation of a role counts the members of a set edge's node | stored |

**Sorts of reference**

A sort is a `graphspec.schema.references.Sort(nodes, edges, least, most, ordered, version)`: whether its members are nodes, edges or both, the least and the most members a task may name, whether their order is kept, and its version. `Sort.columns()` gives the columns its table stores, `Sort.check` checks the members of a graph, `Sort.resolved` gives for each member the observations of its nodes at the time of the task. A task that names under one of its references fewer or more members than the sort of the reference holds stops the build with an error naming the task, its kind, its graph, the reference and what the sort holds. A sort made with other numbers, or a subclass, is registered under a new name and used by a schema like the shipped ones.

| Sort | Members | Stored per member |
|---|---|---|
| `node` | one node, or one set edge where the reference names kinds of set edge | the node or the set edge |
| `edge` | one edge between two nodes | its first node, its relation, the kind and the key of its second node |
| `nodes` | one or more nodes or set edges, without order | the node or the set edge |
| `path` | one or more edges in order, each sharing a node with the one before | as `edge`, and its place from zero |
| `subgraph` | one or more nodes and edges, without order | as `edge`, the relation and the second node missing for a node |
| `graph` | the graph itself | nothing |

An edge named by a reference is of a relation the schema states, between kinds the relation joins; of a relation without direction between two nodes of one kind it is kept with the lower entity first, as facts are. A reference resolves through the view: a node to its observation at the step of the task, an edge to the observations of its two nodes, the graph to everything the step shows.

**Kinds of target**

A kind of target says what a target is, how it is stored and read back, and how its values are scaled. It is registered in `graphspec.TARGETS` under its name, and a name registered twice stops with an error. A target is stored as rows of the table of targets, its points; every point holds a number `y`, its series and the columns its kind stores. An entry states:

| Member | What it states |
|---|---|
| `scaled` | Whether its values are numbers scaled per series. For every series of a kind that scales, the build fits the mean and the sample standard deviation of the targets of the series in the splits trained on, and a fit predicts and is trained on values less that mean over that deviation. The values of a kind that does not scale are read as stated. |
| `matures` | The type of the columns a target of the kind may be read as by later tasks (`matures` of a kind of task), or `None` where it may not. |
| `ripe(column, series, where)` | For a kind that matures, the column of every series of a target, from `column` of what matures as a kind of task states it; a statement of another form stops the schema naming it. |
| `ripened(series, y)` | For a kind that matures, the value as stated that each point, of the series and `y` given, writes into the column of its series, or `None` where it writes nothing. |
| `names` | `None` where its points name no node; `"members"` where each point names, by its key in the column `node`, a member of the reference the target is about, every member of it once; `"linked"` where its one point names a node that the relation of its target joins to the node of the reference it is about. |
| `parameters` | The names its target statement holds beside `kind`, `series`, `about` and `utility`. |
| `checked(stated, schema, references, where)` | Checks `about` and the parameters of a target statement against the schema and its references, and gives the parameters; a statement it refuses stops the schema naming it. |
| `columns` | The columns of the table of targets it stores beside `id`, `y` and `known`, each with its type. Two kinds of target that store one column with two types stop the schema, and so does a column named `id`, `y`, `known`, `task` or `owner`. |
| `inputs` | Those of its columns that are numbers a model may read per point, at 32 bits, beside the series of the point and the node it names, such as `tau` of a path or of a time to an event and `place` of a sequence: the head of the kind reads them, and they reach the batch a model reads at the points of the kinds that state them, missing at every other point. |
| `outcomes` | Those of its columns that are numbers stating the outcome of a target per point, such as `duration` and `happened` of a time to an event: they never reach the batch a model reads, which holds no `y` either; the loss of a fit reads them and `y` from the bank by the rows of the batch, and the measures and `predicted` read them with the inputs. A column is an input or an outcome of a kind, never both. The bank holds inputs and outcomes, for the loss, the measures and `predicted`, in the type of their column, a whole number as a whole number of its width, a flag as a flag and a `FLOAT` at 32 bits, so that they read them as stated; where a point's kind does not store one, a number holds NaN, a whole number 0 and a flag false, which nothing reads. `y` is held at 64 bits, as the table of targets stores it, and the measures and the stored predictions read it so; the loss reads it in the type of the predictions. |
| `times` | Those of its columns that are times in the unit of the graph of the task of the point, such as `tau` of a path and `tau` and `duration` of a time to an event. Each is stored as `INT64`, a whole number of 64 bits, read back as stated and held so by the bank, and the losses and the measures compare its values as whole numbers, exactly; where it is an input, a model reads it at 32 bits, as it reads every input. A time past 2^63 - 1, the greatest whole number of 64 bits, stops the staging of its target naming the task, the column and the value, or the schema where the statement of a kind of task fixes it. Times of graphs of different units are never compared, so a schema that states more than one unit and a kind of task whose target is of a kind that states any stops, naming the kinds of task, the kinds of target and the units. |
| `series(stated, where)` | The series of a target statement, read from its `series` in the form the kind states; `written(series)` gives them back in that form. |
| `points(rows, target, where)` | From the rows of the table of targets of the tasks of one kind of task, `task`, `graph`, `y` and the columns it stores, and the target statement of the kind of task as the schema holds it, `series` and the parameters among it, the checks of the kind and, per row, its series under `series`, each of its inputs and outcomes and, where it names nodes, the key of the node under `node`. |
| `staged(stated, target, where)` | The rows of the table of targets of one target as a script states it, each with `y` and the columns it stores, `target` being the target statement of its kind of task as the schema holds it. |
| `read(rows)` | The target as a script states it, from its rows in their order. |
| `predicted(values, task, fields)` | The predicted target of the points of the kind, in the units of the target, from the values a model gives them per point (in the units stated, for a kind that scales), `task` naming the task of each point and `fields` holding its inputs and outcomes per point. Measures, the stored predictions and utilities read it. |

| Kind | A target, as a script states it | Its `series` | Points | Predicted | Scaled | Matures |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `number` | a finite number | the name of its one series | one, of that series; a task holding two stops the build naming its graph and its kind | the value | yes | into one column of type `number`, as its value |
| `path` | a list of points, each `[tau, series, y]`, `tau` an offset in the unit of the graph of its task, a whole number of at least 1, so the schema refuses it where it states more than one unit | a list of names | one per point, storing `tau INT64` and `series STRING`: `tau` a whole number of at least 1, the series one the kind of task states, no two points of one task with one series and one `tau`; each stops the build naming the graph or the kind | the value | yes | no |
| `class` | one of its classes, by name | its classes, a list of at least two names | one per class, storing `series STRING`, the class, with `y` 1 for the class of the task and 0 for every other; a task without exactly one point per class stops the build naming its graph, and one whose points do not state one 1 and otherwise 0 naming its kind | the probability of each class: the exponential of its value over the sum of those of the classes of its task | no | into one column of type `category`, as its class; the dictionary of the column holds every class |
| `classes` | a set of its classes, as a list of distinct names, possibly empty; read back in the order of the classes | its classes, a list of at least one name | one per class, storing `series STRING`, with `y` 1 for a class of the set and 0 for every other; a task without exactly one point per class stops the build naming its graph, and a `y` other than 0 or 1 naming its kind | the probability of each class on its own, one over one plus the exponential of minus its value | no | into one column of type `flag` per class, true for the classes of the set and false for the others |
| `proportions` | a mapping from every one of its classes to its share, each finite and at least 0, the shares summing to 1 within 1e-6 | its classes, a list of at least two names | one per class, storing `series STRING`, with `y` its share; a task without exactly one point per class stops the build naming its graph, and shares that do not hold as stated naming its kind | the share of each class: the exponential of its value over the sum of those of the classes of its task | no | into one column of type `number` per class, as its share |
| `sequence` | a list of at most `length` of its tokens, by name, possibly empty; its statement states `length`, the greatest length, a whole number of at least 1 | its tokens, its vocabulary, a list of at least one name | one per place from 0 below `length` and per token, storing `series STRING`, the token, and `place INT32`, with `y` 1 for the token at that place of the sequence and 0 for every other, so a place past the end of the sequence holds none; a task without exactly one point per token and place, or a place outside the length, stops the build naming its graph, and points that do not state at most one token per place, the places that hold one coming first, naming its kind | the probability of each token at its place: the exponential of its value over 1 plus the sum of those of the tokens at that place of its task, 1 standing for the end of the sequence, which takes the rest | no | no |
| `survival` | a mapping of `duration`, a whole number of at least 1 in the unit of the graph of its task, the time from the task to its event or to the end of its case, and `happened`, true where its event happened then and false where the case ended first; its statement states `width` and `intervals`, whole numbers of at least 1, the intervals it predicts the event in, the first ending `width` after the time of the task; the schema refuses it where time is not counted on a clock, where it states more than one unit, and where the end of its last interval, `width` times `intervals`, is past 2^63 - 1 | the name of its one series | one per interval, storing `tau INT64`, the end of the interval after the time of the task, and the target, `duration INT64` and `happened BOOL`, on every point, with `y` 1 for the interval in which its event happened, after `tau` less `width` and at or before `tau`, and 0 for every other, so a case that ended first, or whose event happened after the last interval, holds none; a task without one point per interval, or whose points state different targets, stops the build naming its graph, and points whose `y` does not follow from their target naming its kind | the probability that the event happens in each interval: the product over the intervals before it of one less the probability of the event in an interval reached, times that probability for the interval itself, which is one over one plus the exponential of minus its value; the rest is the probability that it happens after the last | no | no |
| `ranking` | a mapping from the key of every item its task lists under the reference it is about, a reference of a sort that holds nodes only, to its relevance, each finite and at least 0, one at least above 0 | the name of its one series | one per item, storing `node STRING`, the key of the item, with `y` its relevance; a point naming a node twice for one task, or no item of a relevance above 0, stops the build naming its graph or its kind; a node that is not one member of the reference of its task, or a task whose points do not name every member, stops it naming the graph, the node and the task | the probability that each item is ranked first: the exponential of its value over the sum of those of the items of its task | no | no |
| `nodes` | a mapping from the key of every node its task lists under the reference it is about, a reference of a sort that holds nodes only, to whether it belongs to the set, true or false | the name of its one series | one per node listed, storing `node STRING`, with `y` 1 for a node that belongs and 0 for one that does not; a point naming a node twice for one task, or a `y` other than 0 or 1, stops the build naming its graph or its kind; the nodes resolve as for a ranking | the probability that each node belongs, one over one plus the exponential of minus its value | no | no |
| `link` | the key of the node its task links to; its statement states `relation`, an edge table that joins the kinds of the reference it is about, of a sort that holds one node, to other kinds, from its sources or, without direction, from either end | the name of its one series | one, storing `node STRING`, with `y` 1; a task holding two points, or a `y` other than 1, stops the build naming its graph or its kind; a key that names no node, or not exactly one node of a kind the relation joins to the kind of the node of the reference, stops it naming the graph, the node and the task | the probability that the task links to each of its candidates, its node and the negatives drawn for it (see Links among sampled negatives): the exponential of its value over the sum of those of the candidates of its task | no | no |

The classes of a kind of target over classes, and the tokens of a sequence, are stated by the schema as its series, never read from the rows: a point stores its class or token as its series, and the head of the kind gives each its own learned embedding. A class named by two kinds of task of one kind of target is one series of both. The places of a sequence are predicted together, each from its task alone, so no prediction reads a token of the target; a sequence does not mature, since a column is written per series and a sequence would need one per place.

A time to an event is predicted as the probability of its event in each interval of `width` from the time of the task, given that it has not happened before: the head of the kind reads the task, its series and an encoding of the `tau` of each interval, never `duration` or `happened`. The loss reads every interval the case lasted through, and the interval in which its event happened: a case that ended first counts the intervals it lasted through and no other, so it never states that its event did not happen. `tau` is the one input of the kind; `duration` and `happened` are its outcomes, read by its loss and its measures, and never in the batch a model reads. The bank holds `duration` and `tau` as whole numbers of 64 bits, as they are stored, and the loss and the measures compare them as such, so that their comparisons agree with `y` for every duration; the head reads `tau` at 32 bits. A time to an event does not mature: its points are intervals, and the truth keeps per point only its series and `y`, from which no column states the outcome of a case that ended first; its event and the end of its case are facts of the graph where the source states them.

The nodes the points of a kind of target name are resolved by the build: the key in the column `node` names, under `members`, one member of the reference the target is about in the task of the point, and under `linked`, one node of the graph of a kind that the relation of the target joins to the kind of the node of that reference; anything else stops the build naming the graph, the node and the task. The truth stores the node under `target_node`, and the bank reads it by the hash of its kind and key, as `entity_token`. A model reads the node of a point as a member of the task of the point under a name of its own, after every reference, observed at the step of the task and embedded by the family's own machinery for a member (see What a family reads); no reference pools it, and only the head of the kind of target reads it, scoring the point from the task and that node. A node that a point names among the members of a reference and that is not observed at the step of its task follows the `absent` of its kind of task: `stop` stops the batch naming the node, the task, its kind and its graph; `keep` gives the head nothing to read for it. The node a `link` names, and the node it starts from, are observed at the step of its task in every view (see Views); no operation keeps either because a link names it. A task whose target is a `link` and whose start or named node the variant does not hold at the step of the task, other than as an anchor, is left out: the batch holds its point with no node and no negative, under `left`, the loss reads none of its points, and the measures leave it out (see Links among sampled negatives), so that every candidate of a task kept passed the same operations. No kind of target that names nodes matures.

**Links among sampled negatives.** The candidates of a task whose target is a `link` are its node and the negatives `graphspec.negatives` drew for it beside the variant: a pipeline that holds one entry of `graphspec.negatives` with `negatives/of` `link:<relation>:<reference>`, the relation and the reference the target states, draws them per task, and a task is matched to the negatives of that entry drawn at its step from the node of its reference. A fit whose pipeline holds no such entry, or two, stops when it starts naming the kind of task. A search never offers such a pipeline: for every kind of task whose target is a link (`graphspec.schema.targets.needed`), it holds present the first entry of `graphspec.negatives` of its template whose bounds admit that `negatives/of`, with that value, and takes the value from the options of every other entry of the operation, holding absent one left no option; a template with no such entry, or bounds that hold present an entry left no option, stop the search when it starts, naming the kind of task or the entry. The draw of the negatives of a task leaves out the node it links to, and the nodes the other tasks drawn from the same node at the same step link to, so every candidate is one node and a task has the `negatives/count` of its entry plus one candidates wherever its rule gives enough nodes, whichever node it links to. Each negative is a point of the batch beside the points of the bank, with `y` 0 and the task, series, kind and weight of the point of the link; the candidates of a task sit in the batch, and their nodes in the table of members, in the order of the tokens of their nodes, whichever of them the link names, so that no place tells the node a task links to from its negatives; the losses and measures of a link read every candidate of its task, and a fit stores the predictions of the negatives under `drawn_row`, the row of the point of their link, and `drawn_prediction`. A task of a link that has fewer candidates than the `negatives/count` of its entry plus one, as where the graph holds too few nodes to draw from, or it is left out because the pipeline did not keep the node it starts from or links to at its step, is never scored among them: the measures of a fit leave it out, and `result.json` holds beside them, under `unmeasured`, the number of such tasks scored on per kind of task whose target is a link. The edge a link names is a fact of the graph where the source states it, and the rule `without_targets` keeps from the tasks of a split every fact about the edges of the relation of a link of the split between the node it starts from and the node it links to, from the time of the task of the link until its target is known.

A kind of target registered from outside the library is used as the shipped ones are, with no change to the library: it is registered in `graphspec.TARGETS`; its kinds of task state a utility that reads it, registered in `graphspec.UTILITIES` where none shipped does; the head a family attaches for it is registered in `tuning.HEADS` under its name; and a fit names for it a loss registered in `tuning.LOSSES` that reads it, summed with the losses of the other kinds of target of the bank, and measures registered in `tuning.MEASURES`, one at least reading it. Each family attaches one head per kind of target the bank holds, and a head reads the inputs of its kind per point and never its outcomes, which no batch a model reads holds (see tuning/README.md). Its entries are carried to every process the library starts as other entries registered from outside are; the registry of each is imported there first.

**Utilities**

A utility is the value of an outcome: a function from a predicted target to one number, with the direction that is better. Every kind of task that states a target states a utility, and the measures that order options read it and nothing else. Utilities are registered in `graphspec.UTILITIES`, each with `reads`, the kinds of target it reads; `parameters`, the names its statement holds beside `name` and `better`; `checked(stated, series, where)`, which checks them against the series of the target and gives them; and `value(stated, points)`, which takes the points of predicted targets, `task`, `series`, `y` and the inputs and outcomes of their kind of target, and gives the tasks in order and one value for each. A target states `utility` as `name`, `better`, `higher` or `lower`, and every parameter of the utility; a utility that does not read the kind of the target, a parameter absent or unknown, or a direction not stated stops the schema naming it. `graphspec.schema.targets.valued(target, points)` gives the utility of the points of a target.

| Utility | Reads | Parameters | The value of a target |
|---|---|---|---|
| `weighted` | `number`, `path`, `class`, `classes`, `proportions`, `sequence`, `survival`, `ranking`, `nodes`, `link` | `weights`, a finite number for every series of the target | the sum over its points of the weight of the series of the point times its `y`: for a class, the value of its class; for a set of classes, the sum of the values of its classes; for a proportion vector, the value of each class weighted by its share; of a predicted target, the value of each class weighted by its probability or its predicted share; for a sequence, the sum of the values of its tokens, and of a predicted sequence their expected sum; for a time to an event, the weight where its event happened within its intervals and 0 otherwise, so 0 for a case that ended first, and of a predicted one the weight times the probability that the event happens within them; for a ranking, the weight times the sum of the relevances stated, and of a predicted ranking the weight, its probabilities summing to 1; for a set of nodes, the weight times the number of nodes that belong, or for a predicted set the number expected; for a link, the weight, its point stating 1 and its predicted probabilities summing to 1 |

**Time**

A fact is one statement about a graph: the values of a node or of a set edge, an edge that holds, an edge stated not to hold. Every fact carries the interval in which it holds, from `since` up to and not including `until`. A time is a whole number counted in the unit its graph states. Times of different graphs are never compared: a kind of target whose values are times (`times` of Kinds of target), whose losses and measures compare them across tasks, is refused in a schema that states more than one unit; no other shipped kind of target holds a time, and no shipped measure reads one but through these kinds.

| Model | The facts of a graph carry | The graph states |
|---|---|---|
| `none` | no time; every fact holds always, and a task has no time | no unit |
| `order` | `since` and `until` as positions in one order; nothing is counted between two positions | no unit |
| `clock` | `since` and `until` | one of the units of the schema |
| `clocks` | `since` and `until`, the clock of what is true, and `known` and `replaced`, the clock of what is known | one of the units of the schema |

- `since` missing means the fact held before every time; `until` missing means no end is stated.
- `known` is the time from which the fact was known; missing, it is `since`. `replaced` is the time from which the fact was replaced by a correction; missing, it never was. The correction is a fact of its own, known from that time. The bank stores `known` and `replaced` only for the facts where they differ from `since` and from never.
- A fact may name the observers that can see it, as a list of names; a fact that names none is seen by all. A task may name the one observer who is looking; a task that names none sees every fact.
- Several facts about one entity may hold at one time, and an edge may hold several times between the same two nodes.

A fact is visible to a task at a time shown when it holds at the time shown, was known no later than the task's time, was not replaced at or before the task's time, names the task's observer or none, and is not kept from the split of the task, at the time of looking, by a rule the fit states. `graphspec.views.visible.visible`, `graphspec.views.visible.allowed` and `graphspec.views.visible.withheld` are the one place that decides this. A task seen as an event is not a fact: it is read only by the tasks of later steps, as What a family reads states, never by a task of its own step.

**Rules for ties**

Facts with the same time are ordered by the rule the schema names. Rules are registered in `graphspec.TIES`; a rule states the column it reads and gives the keys to order by.

| Rule | Reads | Orders by |
|---|---|---|
| `sequence` | `seq INT32`, stated by every fact | `seq` |
| `key` | nothing | the relation, then the key of the source, then the key of the target |

Order matters only between facts about one entity. Two facts about one node or one set edge, or two facts about one edge, of one relation between the same two ends and both present or both absent, that hold from the same time and that the rule leaves equal stop the build with an error naming the node and its kind or the edge, the graph and the time. With no rule named, any two such facts that hold from the same time stop it. Facts about different entities or different edges are never compared: a graph without time and without a rule for ties holds any number of edges, each stated once.

**Shorthand forms**

Forms are registered by name in `graphspec.FORMS`, each a `graphspec.build.forms.Form` made of a function from rows to facts and of whether it counts on a clock. `graphspec.build.forms.reduced(name, model, rows, **parameters)` reduces rows stated in a form to facts with `since` and `until`. A form registered from outside is used the same way, and a name registered twice stops with an error.

| Form | The rows state | Parameters | Each row holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `state` | `since`, `until` | none | over the interval stated |
| `event` | `at` | none | from `at` until the next whole time |
| `snapshot` | `at`, and `seq` where the rule for ties reads it | `times`, every time at which a snapshot was taken | from the first snapshot that states it until the first later snapshot that does not; with no end when the last snapshot states it |
| `change` | `at` | `keys`, the columns that say what changes | from `at` until the next change of the same thing; with no end after the last |
| `signal` | `index`, the number of the reading from zero | `start`, `stride` | from `start` plus `index` times `stride`, for one `stride`; it counts on a clock |

Snapshots that state `seq` must keep one order of their facts over time; a snapshot that orders two facts against an earlier one stops with an error naming its time.

**Tasks**

A task is one row of the table of tasks of its graph. It states its kind, its time unless the model is `none`, the observer who is looking where the schema states observers, its key, its grouping where one is stated, the features of its kind, its signals where the schema states a width, its weight where its kind states one, its split where it holds a target, and under each reference of its kind the members the sort of the reference holds. A task of a kind that states a target may hold one, in the form its kind of target states. A target states the time from which it is known, or none; that time is later than the time of its task, so that a target never enters the view of its own task.

- Several tasks of one graph may share a time, and a time at which facts change may hold no task.
- A split is any name. A task that holds a target states one; a task that holds none may state none.
- A target whose kind states what matures is read, from the time it is known, by later tasks as the values of the stated columns of the node it names, each point writing the value its kind of target gives into the column of its series, until the next target that writes the same column of the same node is known. It is shown only to the observer of its task, or to every observer when its task names none, and only in the views of a split that may see its task: a target of a split trained on in the views of every split, and a target of any other split in the views of its own split only. A view that holds targets of a split trained on, such as the one the splits without rules share when a split trained on states no rule, shows the matured targets of the splits trained on and of no other. A task never sees its own target, and a target not yet known is not shown.

**Tables of a source**

A source is a staging directory of Parquet tables or a database holding the same tables. The columns of time of a fact table are those of the model: none, `since INT64` and `until INT64`, and with two clocks `known INT64` and `replaced INT64`; then the column the rule for ties reads; then `observers STRING[]` when the schema states observers.

| Table | Kind | Columns | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `graph` | node | `id INT32` key, `name STRING`, `unit STRING` when the model has a clock, the columns of the graph | One row per graph. Names are unique. |
| one per kind of node and per kind of set edge, named as the kind | node | `id INT64` key, `graph INT32`, `entity INT32`, `key STRING` | The entities of each graph: its nodes and its set edges. `entity` is unique within its graph. |
| `observation` | node | `id INT64` key, the columns of time | One fact about one node or one set edge. |
| `observed_<kind>` | relationship | FROM the kind TO `observation`; the columns of the kind | The values the fact states. |
| `member_<kind of set edge>` | relationship | FROM the kind of set edge TO each kind of its members; `role STRING`; the member columns | One member of a set edge. |
| `edge_<table>` | relationship | FROM and TO the endpoint kinds stated, kinds of set edge among them; `present BOOLEAN`; the columns of time; the relation column `STRING` when the schema states one; the columns of the table | Facts about edges. `present = false` states that the edge does not hold. |
| `held_<kind>`, for every kind that holds graphs | node | `id INT64` key, `graph INT32`, `owner STRING`, the key of the node that holds it, `kind STRING`, its held kind, `key STRING`, the columns of every held kind of the kind | One held node. A column its held kind does not state is missing. |
| `held_edge_<kind>` | relationship | FROM and TO `held_<kind>`; `relation STRING`; the columns of every held relation of the kind | One held edge. A column its relation does not state is missing. |
| `task` | node | `id INT64` key, `graph INT32`, `kind STRING`, `time INT64` unless the model is `none`, `observer STRING` when the schema states observers, `split STRING`, `weight FLOAT`, the grouping column `INT32` when one is stated, the key column `INT64`, the columns of every kind of task, and with signals `signal FLOAT[S]` and `signal_observed FLOAT[S]` | One task. A column its kind does not state is missing. |
| `task_<reference>` | relationship, for every reference whose sort stores members | FROM `task` TO the kinds the reference states; with edges `relation STRING`, `to_kind STRING`, `to_key STRING`; with order `place INT32` | One member of what a task refers to under that name. |
| `target` | node | `id INT64` key, `y DOUBLE`, `known INT64` unless the model is `none`, and the columns every kind of target the schema states stores | One point of a target. A column its kind of target does not store is missing; a point that states one stops the build naming its kind of task and the column. |
| `target_of` | relationship | FROM `target` TO `task` | The task a number belongs to. |

A table the schema states and the source lacks, a column of a table that the schema does not state, a column stored as another type than stated, and a relationship joining other kinds than stated each stop with an error naming it.

- Names of kinds, columns, edge tables, references, roles and kinds of task hold no `:`. A kind of node or of set edge is not named `graph`, `observation`, `task`, `target`, `target_of` or `step` and does not start with `observed_`, `task_`, `edge_`, `member_` or `held_`. A relation is not named `diffusion`, `rewire`, `temporal` or `untyped`; those belong to derived relations. Member columns are not named `role`; held columns are not named `id`, `graph`, `owner`, `kind`, `key` or `relation`.
- The relations of an edge table that takes them from a column are the distinct values of that column over the whole source, in sorted order. Each has its own code and the endpoints, direction and columns of its table. The table is one relation with a value: a pipeline keeps, drops, filters and projects it by the parameters of the table, `relations/<table>/keep` and `edges/<table>/`; the values of its edges are stored in one array; a model holds the weights of the table once; and the value of each edge reaches every family that reads edges through a learned embedding of its code (see What a family reads). A row without a value in that column stops the build. A bank holds at most 32,767 relations stated or taken. An edge table is not named `diffusion`, `rewire`, `temporal` or `untyped`, nor as a relation of a set edge.
- An edge of a table without direction joins its two ends in no order. Between two entities of one kind it is kept with the lower entity first.
- `id` keys are unique across a whole table and increase down its rows. A staging reader resolves ids by position when they are dense from zero, or when each graph's ids are its index shifted left by 32 bits plus its row within the graph.
- The tasks of a graph are kept in order of their time, tasks of one time in the order of their rows. The key of a task is unique within its graph.
- A present fact about an edge carries a value in each of its columns.
- Every `y` is finite at float32 precision.

**Bank version 13**

`tuning.build_bank(task, output, workers, trained)` reads the source twice: once to find the dictionaries, which hold every value stated and every class of a target that matures into a column of categories, the relations taken from columns and the names of the observers, and once to write the graphs. `trained` names the splits the scaling of the targets is fitted on, each a split the source holds; a fit trains on exactly these. Every series of a kind of target that scales needs two targets that differ in those splits. No encoder is fitted by the build. Every graph written is also shown once under the shipped choice of steps `tasks`, or `changes` for a graph that holds no task, with the shipped rule `first` for several facts where the bank holds them, with every kind of set edge under the form `node`, which shows every fact, member and edge about a set edge, and with no rule stated for any split, so that a graph the operations cannot read stops the build; what else is registered does not change it. A bank of another version is refused with an error naming the version found.

A source with kinds of set edge or held graphs is read from a staging directory; a database source stating either stops with an error naming them.

`manifest.json` holds `version` 13, `task`, `trained` (the splits the scaling of the targets was fitted on), `splits` (the names of the splits, sorted), `split_rows` (the number of rows of each), `observers` (the names of the observers, sorted), `signals`, `graphs`, `spec` and `stored_sha256`, the digest of what the build stored: the digest of every file under `truth/` and of `supervision.json`, by its path in the bank, with every field of the manifest but `database`, the place of the source, and the seconds the build and each graph took. An identical rebuild gives the same digest, and a rebuild that stores other graphs or tables another; caches key a bank by it, never by a time. An entry of `graphs` holds the index and name of a graph, its `unit`, and its numbers of entities, of facts about nodes and set edges (`observations`), of facts about edges (`facts`), of members of set edges (`members`), of held nodes (`held`), of tasks and of targets. `spec` is the declaration of the registry: `schema`, the mapping above; `encoding`, with `dictionaries` per category column, `states` null, and `relations` per edge table; the relations with their codes; and `extras`. The kinds of target of a bank and its series are read from its schema, `Schema.targets()` and `Schema.series()`, in the order the kinds of task state them, and a schema without a target stops the registry. The width of the encoded table, of its value part and of each kind of node and of set edge belong to the encoders fitted with the views of a statement.

| Extra | Holds |
|---|---|
| `signals` | The width of the signals. |
| `base` | The relations stated or taken, then the relations of the forms of storage of every kind of set edge. |
| `events` | Whether a kind of task is seen as an event. |
| `several` | Whether a shipped choice of steps can show two facts about one node or set edge of a graph of the bank at one step: two whose intervals overlap, or, under `tasks`, two that hold from after one time of a task of an observer up to and including its next, the earlier an event and the later an event or still holding at that next time, where the times of tasks are those of every task of the observer, or those of the tasks a split with rules would be shown with: the tasks of the observer of the split, of the splits `trained` names and of no split, for every split the source states, since any split may be given rules. Times of tasks are those the source states; when a fact is known, whether it was replaced and who may see it are not read. |
| `untasked` | The names of the graphs that hold no task, in the order of the source. |
| `unvalued` | For every kind of node a reference can name, by its kinds, by the ends of the edge tables where its sort holds edges, or as the node a target names, and every column of the kind: under `<kind>:<column>`, the number of nodes of the kind in the graphs of the bank that hold no value of the column in some fact about them, where it is above zero. `graphspec.contract` needs a grouping not named here (see Operations). |

`supervision.json` holds `rows` and `split`. There is one row per point of a target, in the order of the `target` table. A row holds `graph`, `kind`, the key of its task and, where one is stated, its grouping, under the names the schema states, `time` unless the model is `none`, `weight` where the kind states one, `known` where the target states one, `series`, the series of the point, the inputs and outcomes of its kind of target, such as `tau` for a path, `node`, the key of the node the point names where its kind of target names nodes, and `y`. `split` holds `indices`, the rows of every split by name, and `series`, a list with one entry for every series of a kind of target that scales, in the order of the series of the schema, of `name`, `mean` and `sd` over the rows of the splits trained on. Every such series needs two differing targets in those splits.

`truth/graph_NNNN/` holds one graph as arrays of facts, `graphspec.facts.stored.Facts`, with `graph.json` giving its name, the keys of its entities, its unit, under `inner` the keys of its held nodes and under `observers` the names of the observers of the bank, which views and their errors name.

**Views**

Steps are not stored. The operations read a view: the facts of a graph shown at chosen times, as the tasks of each time saw them. `graphspec.operations.sets.stored(facts, registry, storage)` applies the form of storage the pipeline states for every kind of set edge; `graphspec.views.view.sequences(facts, registry, steps, looking)` gives the sequences of the graph it returns and `graphspec.views.view.view(facts, registry, sequence, several)` shows one as stated, a `graphspec.views.graph.Shown`, applying the rule of every edge about a set edge; `graphspec.views.view.finished(shown, registry, held)` encodes it with fitted encoders into a `graphspec.views.graph.Graph`. The pipeline chooses them by its settings, and the statement of the fit by what each split may see.

| Setting | Choices | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `time/steps` | registered in `graphspec.STEPS`; shipped: `tasks`, `changes` | `tasks`: one step per time at which a task sits, shown as the tasks of that time saw it. `changes`: one step per time at which a fact starts, ends, becomes known or is replaced, or at which a task sits. Under both, the tasks of each observer form a sequence of their own, and so do the tasks that name none. A graph that holds no task is shown by `changes` in one sequence that names no observer, and not at all where no fact of it starts or ends at a time; `tasks` cannot show it and stops naming the graph and the choice. A search offers a choice of steps only where it shows every graph of the bank, so it offers `tasks` only where the extra `untasked` names no graph, and bounds that leave it no choice stop the search naming the graphs and the choices; a configuration stated in full is not narrowed. A choice states `serves(registry)`, whether it is offered for the bank at all, and `refusal(registry)`, nothing where it shows every graph of the bank and otherwise why it does not, naming the graphs. A step covers the times after the step before it up to and including its own. An event, a fact that holds from a time until the next whole time, is shown at the first step at or after its time, where the step's tasks know it; any other fact is shown at the steps whose time it holds at. |
| `time/several` | registered in `graphspec.SEVERAL`; shipped: `first`, `last`; no default | Which of several facts about one node or set edge shown at one step the view shows, in the order of the time they hold from, then of the rule for ties. The parameter exists only where the extra `several` of the bank holds, and a configuration that does not state it then stops naming it. Where it does not hold, no shipped choice of steps shows two facts about one node at one step, and a view that does, under a choice registered from outside, stops naming the graph, the time and the node; nothing is chosen without a stated rule. |
| `sets/<kind>/storage`, per kind of set edge | the forms of `graphspec.STORAGE` offered for the kind; no default | The form of storage of the set edges of the kind. |

A choice of steps is called with the facts of one graph, the registry and the tasks it shows, and gives one sequence per observer of those tasks: the observer, the times it shows, the time of looking at each, its tasks and the step of each; the times of tasks it is not given shape none of them. A step may hold no task and may hold several. A choice that leaves a task it is given that holds a target without a step stops with an error.

What the tasks of a split may see is a list of rules, registered in `graphspec.SEES`; a rule is a function of the facts, the registry and the sequence that gives, for the facts about nodes and then for the facts about edges, the intervals over which each is kept from the split, as three arrays of whole numbers of one length: the fact, by its row, the time from which and the time until which it is kept; a fact kept over several intervals appears once per interval, and one kept over none does not appear; minus two to the power 62 stands for a time before every time and two to the power 62 for no end. A step whose time of looking falls within an interval of a fact does not show the fact; the steps before and after it show the fact as the clock does. A rule that gives anything else stops naming it. A name registered twice stops with an error.

| Rule | Keeps from the tasks of the split |
|---|---|
| `without_targets` | Every fact that reveals a target of the split: for every task of the split that holds a target whose kind states the reference it is about, the facts about the edges that reference names, and where it names a set edge, the facts about the set edge and every fact about an edge from or to it; for every task of the split whose target is a link, the facts about the edges of its relation between the node of its reference and the node it links to, in either order where the relation has no direction; each while the target is not yet known, from the time of its task until the time from which its target is known, the latest of its points, or at every later time where the target states none; so that what held and was known before the task stays visible, and once the target is known the fact is shown as the clock shows any other. Any other target about a node or about none of its references keeps nothing. |
| `without_scored` | At every time, every fact about a node that only tasks of the splits scored on name, and every fact about an edge of such a node. It is stated for splits trained on. |

A split for which rules are stated is shown in a sequence of its own, per observer: as tasks its own, those of the splits trained on and those that name no split, at the steps the choice of steps gives for those tasks alone, so that the tasks it does not keep, such as those of a split scored on that its rules hide, shape neither its steps nor its windows; the facts its rules leave; and the targets of its own tasks. The splits without rules share one sequence, at the steps the choice gives for every task of the observer, which holds their tasks, those of the splits trained on and those that name no split. Matured targets are shown in them as the section Tasks states.

A view never shows a time later than the time of looking at it. The facts about edges that hold at one step are listed in the order of the rule for ties, and facts the rule leaves equal by the time they hold from. A step of a view shows the facts as its own tasks saw them; a later task reading earlier steps does not see there what became known afterwards.

A view is a `graphspec.views.graph.Graph`: spans over its steps (`obs_start`, `obs_stop`, `fact_start`, `fact_stop`, `fact_rank` with the patches `order_step`, `order_position`, `order_span`, and the absent facts as `removal_*`) with the values as stated in `obs_values` and encoded in `obs_x`; `entity_held`, per entity the encodings of the graph it holds in the blocks of the held graphs, zero where it holds none or where the view shows it at no step; its tasks with `task_step`, the step of each, `task_key`, `task_kind`, `task_values`, `task_x`, their columns and the attributes of their graph encoded, and `task_signals`; the members of their references as in the bank, `member_task` numbering the tasks of the view; its targets as `label_task` and `label_row`; `period`, the group of every step; `step_time`, the time each step shows, in the unit of the graph; and `step_token`, the 64-bit hash of the name of the graph shown, at every step. `period` holds the grouping the tasks of the step state where the schema names one, and otherwise numbers the steps; groups are runs of equal values and need not increase. Where the schema names a grouping, every step holds a task and the tasks of one step state one group. A view may show no edge at all, as where the schema states no relation or the rules of a split hide every edge. A view needs at least one step, values of both ends of a present edge at every step it is shown, where they lack an error names the edge, its relation, the end without a value, its kind and the observer by name, where an edge about a set edge follows its rule, for the tasks of a kind that states `absent` as `stop`, values of every node a reference names at the step of the task, and for a task whose target the view holds and is a `link`, values of the node its link starts from and of the node it links to at the step of the task. The members of a view are those of its tasks' references and, for every target the view holds that is a `link`, the node it links to, as a member of its task named one past the references of the schema, after the task's other members, so that `graphspec.negatives` leaves it out of the negatives of its task and the variant tells whether the pipeline kept it; no operation keeps it for being named, and the members a family reads leave it out. A bank keeps its views beside its variants in a folder of the runtime home keyed by the digest of what its build stored, `stored_sha256` of its manifest, and the version of the code of views, so that other code never reads them (see The key of a variant), under `views/<digest of the settings of time, the forms of storage, what the splits may see and the name and version of every registered entry the views read>/graph_NNNN/`, each holding `graph.json` and every array of the view in one file, `arrays.pack`, with `index.json` naming for each the graph it shows, its observer and its split, -1 for the sequence the splits without rules share, and `encoders.json` holding under `states` the fitted state of every column, null for a column no view of the statement shows. The views are built in two passes over the bank: the first shows as stated the views in which a split trained on is trained and gathers what they show, the encoders are fitted on it, and the second shows every view again and encodes it with them, so that one view at a time is held. `tuning.bank.bank.Deriver.fitted(cfg)` gives the registry of the pipeline of `cfg`: the registry of the bank with the encoders fitted for its statement, grown by what its operations add (see What an operation adds); its widths, kinds and relations are those models read.

In the form the operations work on, `Graph.dense()`, `step_graph` names for every step the graph it came from when graphs are joined, `step_time` and `step_token` stay with their steps through joins, slices and segments, and `x_table` and `value_table` hold the rows that `obs_ref` and `fact_ref` point to.

| Name in version 7 | In version 8 |
|---|---|
| the two names of scaling of a single target | one entry of the `series` list |
| the row keys that named a level of time and a query | the grouping and the key of the tasks, under the names the schema states |
| row key `step`, columns `step` of the tables | `time` of tasks, `since` and `until` of facts |
| facts restated at every step, and the arrays of spans over steps | facts with intervals; the spans are the arrays of a view |
| the array that names, for every step of joined graphs, the graph the step came from | `step_graph` |
| the three arrays of roles of a query, the arrays of queries and of labels | the table of tasks with its members and its targets |
| the two lists of rows of the training and the validation split | `indices`, the rows of every split by name |
| `width` and `features` of one vector for every kind | `widths` per kind, `values`, `features` |

Version 9 adds to the truth the array `target_node`, the node each point of a target names; a bank of version 8 is refused with an error naming its version. Version 10 reckons the extra `several` over the tasks a split with rules is shown with as well as over every task of an observer; a bank of version 9 is refused with an error naming its version. Version 11 stores every column a kind of target lists under `times`, `tau` of a path and of a time to an event, as a whole number of 64 bits, in the table of targets and in `supervision.json`; a bank of version 10 is refused with an error naming its version. Version 12 adds to the manifest `stored_sha256`, the digest of what the build stored; a bank of version 11 is refused with an error naming its version. Version 13 adds the extra `unvalued`; a bank of version 12 is refused with an error naming its version.

**Operations**

An operation takes a graph and parameters and returns a graph; an operation that takes two graphs also reads a second graph beside the one it changes (see Second graphs). Its input is never changed, and every array it leaves unchanged is the same object in its output, so an operation that changes nothing returns its input. An operation is a `graphspec.Operation(namespace, name, phases, parameters, reads, splits, requires, changes, adds, kinds, version, graphs)`, registered in `graphspec.OPERATIONS` under its namespace and name joined by a dot, as `OPERATIONS.register(operation.name, operation)`; a name registered twice stops with an error naming it. Every field is stated:

| Field | What it states |
|---|---|
| `namespace`, `name` | Words without a dot; the library's operations are in the namespace `graphspec` |
| `phases` | A tuple of at least one `graphspec.Phase(name, function, carries, parts)`, of distinct names, run in order on every segment of a derivation (see Derivation). `function(graph, registry, settings, seed, limits, carry, held)` receives the operation's input, in the form the operations work on, `Graph.dense()`, and `held`, what the phase before it handed on (none for the first), and hands on its result; the last phase hands on the output graph. `carries` is none where the phase reads no state carried from the segments before, so that it may run on segments in any order; otherwise a function from the registry and the settings to the state it carries into the first segment, which the phase reads and replaces as `carry.state`. `parts` is none, or a function from the registry and the settings to the names of parts the phase runs apart, each with a state of its own and `carry.part` naming it, so that parts may run side by side; the next phase receives their results as a mapping by name. The last phase is not run in parts, and no phase run in parts follows another |
| `parameters` | A function from a registry to its parameters, each a `graphspec.operations.parameters.Parameter` with its range, its default and the condition under which it is active |
| `reads` | The names of the operations whose parameters the conditions of its own parameters read; in a pipeline they are read from the entry of each that states no label, and an operation the pipeline does not hold has none |
| `splits` | The splits it may read, among `trained` and `scored`; of the shipped operations `graphspec.negatives` reads both, for the tasks whose targets the view it derives holds, and the others read none |
| `requires` | Its counts, above, and what it needs of a bank, each a `graphspec.operations.operation.Need(parameter, lacking)`: `parameter` is none, for what it needs whatever its parameters, or the name of a parameter whose every value needs something of the bank; `lacking(registry, value)`, with `value` none where no parameter is named, gives none where the bank holds what it needs and otherwise says what it needs and the bank lacks. A search holds absent an entry whose operation the bank cannot serve, or which the bank leaves no value of a parameter, and never offers a value the bank cannot serve; a pipeline that holds one is refused when stated, naming the entry, the operation or the parameter and its value, and what the bank lacks. Shipped: `graphspec.consecutive` needs a relation stated without columns from a kind of node to itself, `graphspec.nearby` a column of numbers stated by two kinds of node or by one, `graphspec.contract` a kind of node with a column and, for each value of `contract/grouping`, a value of its column on every node of its kind that a reference can name, read from the extra `unvalued` of the bank, since a reference to a node without one cannot follow it into a group, `graphspec.product` a relation, `graphspec.negatives` a relation or a kind of set edge. It also holds, each a `graphspec.operations.operation.Stored(parameter, kinds)`, the values of a parameter that need the set edges of kinds stored as nodes, `kinds(registry, value)` giving those kinds: a search draws the pipeline after the forms of storage and offers no such value where the form drawn for one of its kinds makes no node; bounds that hold an entry present with no value left that the forms of storage they allow can serve stop the search when it starts, naming the entry, the parameter, the setting and the form; a statement whose settings store such a kind otherwise is refused, naming the entry, the parameter, its value, the setting and its form. Shipped: `negatives/of` of `graphspec.negatives`, whose `set:<kind>` needs the set edges of the kind stored as nodes |
| `changes` | What it changes among `time`, `observations` and `facts`, and `references` where it may remove a referenced node |
| `adds` | None, or a function from a registry, the settings and the name of its entry to what it adds (see What an operation adds) |
| `kinds` | None, or a function from a registry and the settings, of which it reads choices and flags and never a number, to what the entry does with kinds of node the registry declares, a mapping from each to `names`, where the entry derives for that kind, `removes`, where it names the kind and leaves no node of it, or `fills`, where it may bring nodes of it. A pipeline in which an entry names or removes a kind that an entry before it removed, with no entry between them that fills it, stops when it is stated, with an error naming the entry, the kind and the entry that removed it. A kind of task whose target names nodes states the kinds of node it names (`graphspec.schema.targets.pointed`): those of the reference it is about for `members`, and those its relation joins to the kinds of that reference for `linked`; a pipeline in which an entry removes such a kind stops when it is stated, with an error naming the entry, the kind and the kind of task, whatever entry comes after it |
| `version` | A whole number from 1, raised whenever its code changes; it is part of the key of every variant it made |
| `graphs` | 1, or 2 where it reads a second graph, which its phases find as `carry.second` |

The function receives the spec as `settings`, which hold the parameters the operation states and reads and no other: reading another parameter stops with an error naming it and the operation, and so does reading one the spec does not state. `name in settings` says whether a parameter is active under its condition, and stops where the spec states it while inactive or lacks it while active; no parameter is read with a default. `settings.split(name)` gives the tasks of the graph whose targets are in the splits trained on (`trained`) or scored on (`scored`); an operation reads only the splits it states, and reading another stops with an error naming the operation and the split. `graphspec.operations.operation.listed(registry)` lists every registered operation with its parameters, what it reads, requires and changes, its phases with whether each carries state and runs in parts, and its version.

A phase reads the segment it works on through `carry`: `carry.glob`, the number of every entity of the segment among the entities of the derivation; `carry.step0`, the step of the derivation at which the segment begins; `carry.keys(code, source, target)`, a key of facts equal across segments; `carry.local(entities)`, the places in the segment of entities named by their numbers in the derivation, which stops where one is absent; `carry.widened(graph, entities)`, which adds to the segment the entities its state brings and gives the graph with them and the new place of every entity it held; and `carry.entry`, the name of its entry (see What an operation adds). An entity whose `entity_connected` is set may appear in a later segment; the state carries it, and only it. The state is replaced, never changed in place: the state of a segment is shared by every derivation that continues from it.

**What an operation adds**

An operation that adds what the bank does not state says what in `adds`, which gives a `graphspec.schema.added.Added(columns, kinds, placed, relations, joins, candidates)`: `columns`, the columns it adds, each with its `type` and its `encoder`, of a type whose values are numbers; `kinds`, the kinds of node it adds, each with its `columns` and `tokens`, a function from the kinds, the tokens and the encoded rows of the observations of a graph, and the registry, to the tokens of the nodes of the kind in that graph; `placed`, columns it adds placed on kinds of node the registry holds; `relations`, the relations it adds, each with its `pairs` of kinds, whether it is `directed`, and its `columns`; `joins`, pairs of kinds it joins to relations the registry holds; and `candidates`, the relations it adds as candidates rather than as structure, each with its `pairs` of kinds and whether it is `directed`. A relation of candidates is no relation of the registry: `Registry.candidates` and `Registry.by_candidate` hold it apart, with codes of its own from 0, so no operation offers or reads it, no representation and no family reads it, and the codes, weights and parameters of the relations are those of the pipeline without it. An operation hands what it draws of it to `carry.candidate(code, step, source, target)` and leaves the graph without it; a variant whose registry holds candidates keeps them beside its arrays in `candidate_code`, `candidate_step`, `candidate_source` and `candidate_target`, in the steps and entities of the derivation, ordered by step, code, source and target, as they were drawn whatever the entries after the one that drew them do. What it adds is named after its entry, the label of the entry or the name of its operation without the namespace, which a phase reads as `carry.entry`; a name the registry holds, a kind or a column no one states, or a column placed nowhere stops naming the entry. What is added depends only on the registry and the operation's own parameters, so it is known before anything is derived: `graphspec.operations.pipeline.additions(registry, steps)` lists the additions of a pipeline by entry, and `graphspec.operations.pipeline.grown(registry, steps, states)` gives the registry of the pipeline, every step adding to the registry the steps before it gave. Its kinds of node follow those the bank declares, `Registry.declared`; its relations follow those of the bank, with codes after theirs and reverse codes after those, and its added relations hold values like tables do, `attributes_<relation>` in a variant; the relations every kind may hold (hops, diffusion, rewiring, untyped, temporal and boundaries) hold the added kinds too; its columns follow every column of the encoded table, their values after its values and their flags after its flags, so that the rows of the views are widened by zeros where the derivation begins and the registry's `features` count them. The parameters of the shipped operations and settings read what the bank declares: `graphspec.nodes` keeps every node of an added kind, `graphspec.derive` completes and anchors declared kinds, `graphspec.relations` and `graphspec.edges` keep and filter the relations of the bank, and joining unifies declared kinds. A pipeline is stated against the registry of the bank: `pipeline.resolved(registry, entries)` checks the parameters of each entry against that registry grown by the entries before it, each after the entries it reads and the entries before it that add to the registry, so that `graphspec.product` may name in its path a relation an earlier entry added, which a search does not offer; given a registry that already holds what the pipeline adds, it stops naming the first addition it meets again. The workers of a derivation take the steps the calling process resolved, `pipeline.rebuilt(entries)`.

An operation writes the values of a column it adds into rows it adds to the tables of values, `graphspec.operations.rows.made_rows(graph, carry, rows)` for nodes, encoded by `rows.written(registry, rows, columns, codes, dtype, named)`, which stops past the limit of 16 bits naming the row and the column, and `rows.made_values(graph, carry, values)` for facts. The engine places the rows a segment made after every row of the graphs and of the segments before it, so where a row sits depends on where segments are cut and what each observation and fact reads does not. The nodes of an added kind are numbered before every entity of the graphs of a derivation, one for each token its `tokens` gives for any graph of the derivation or its second graphs, so that a node is keyed by its kind and token: in a joined group it is one entity across the graphs, unified by its token as a connected entity is, and equal to the node the physical join of the graphs gives. Every segment holds them, and they are known to every segment after the first of the derivation. A node an edit brings to a kind that an operation contracts has a node of its group only where some graph given to the derivation held that group.

The encoders of added columns are fitted on what the views of the splits trained on give: `tuning.bank.bank.Deriver.fitted(cfg)` derives with the pipeline every view whose targets include one of a split trained on, up to and including its last step with such a target, the added columns held as their values, gathers the values of every added column on the observations of the kinds and the facts of the relations that hold it, fits the encoder each states on them, keeps the states under `added/<digest>.json` with the encoders of the views, and gives the registry of the pipeline with them. The digest holds every version the key of a variant holds (see The key of a variant): the pipeline with the version of every operation, the device, the seed of derivation, the versions of the code of views, derivation and representations, and the name and version of every registered entry the views and the pipeline read; it leaves out the representations a model reads, which the states are fitted without.

The shipped operations are `graphspec.periods`, `graphspec.window`, `graphspec.nodes`, `graphspec.derive`, `graphspec.relations`, `graphspec.edges`, `graphspec.relation_forms`, `graphspec.nearby`, `graphspec.consecutive`, `graphspec.neighbourhood`, `graphspec.measures`, `graphspec.contract`, `graphspec.product`, `graphspec.negatives`, and `graphspec.merge` and `graphspec.difference`, which take two graphs. Each reads the graph it is given: `graphspec.derive` and `graphspec.product` derive from the relations their input holds, so a relation removed before them is not read and one removed after them is. `graphspec.product` names no kind: its relation joins the kinds the relations of its path join in the registry of the pipeline when it runs; `graphspec.negatives` names the kind of set edge it draws negative sets of, and no kind for links. `graphspec.nodes` names a kind it filters, `graphspec.derive` the kinds it projects, completes or completes within, `graphspec.nearby` its two kinds, `graphspec.consecutive` the kind of the relation it fills, `graphspec.neighbourhood` the kind it keeps the surroundings of; `graphspec.nodes` removes a kind it does not keep, as no node of it is left other than as an anchor of a reference, and `graphspec.contract` removes the kind it contracts; `graphspec.merge` fills every kind. The conditions of the parameters of `graphspec.edges` read those of `graphspec.derive`, which offer the filters of the relations it derives; every other condition reads only the parameters of its own operation.

**Pipelines**

A variant is made by a pipeline, which a configuration states in two parts. A configuration without `pipeline` stops a fit with an error naming it.

- `pipeline`: the operations in the order they apply, as a list of entries. An entry states `operation`, a registered name, and `parameters`, every parameter of the operation that is active under what the entry states, and may state `label`, a word without a slash or a dot. An entry of an operation that takes two graphs states `graph`, the name of the graph of the bank it reads as its second graph, and no other entry states one; the key of a variant holds it with the entry. An entry is named by its label, or else by its operation; an operation stated more than once states a label on every entry of it but one, and no two names are equal. A parameter active and absent, inactive and stated, unknown or out of its range stops with an error naming the entry and the parameter.
- `graph`: the settings, the rest of what makes a variant and what packing reads: `sets/<kind>/storage`, the forms of storage, which act on the stored facts before steps are chosen; `time/steps` and `time/several`, which choose the view; `graphs/join`, `graphs/join_size`, `graphs/connect/<kind>` and `graphs/connect_fraction`, which join graphs; `nodes/untyped` and `relations/direction`, which say how models read kinds and directed relations; the parameters of the groups of representations the model reads; and `time/history`, `time/context`, `neighbors/order` and `neighbors/decay`, read when packing. `graphspec.operations.parameters.settings(registry, groups)` gives them; every setting active is stated, and none is filled from a default.

```python
pipeline = [
    dict(operation="graphspec.periods", parameters={"time/periods": "every", "time/period_length": 4}),
    dict(operation="graphspec.window", parameters={"time/scheme": "window", "time/unit": "step", "time/window": 2}),
    dict(operation="graphspec.relations", parameters={"relations/near/keep": False, "relations/serves/keep": True}),
    dict(operation="graphspec.window", label="late", parameters={"time/scheme": "state"}),
]
```

`graphspec.operations.pipeline.resolved(registry, pipeline)` checks a pipeline and gives its steps, which a derivation applies in the order stated. The state a step carries from segment to segment is its own, so an operation stated twice carries two states.

A search states a template, the entries in order with their labels and second graphs and without parameters, by `--pipeline`, and keeps it in `plan.json`; a continued run states no other. The search chooses which entries are present and their parameters, never their order. `graphspec.operations.pipeline.space(registry, template)` names the presence of the entry N `pipeline/N` and its parameters `pipeline/N/<parameter>`, so the names of an entry stay the same when another is added; bounds and random parameters name them so. A search never offers a pipeline its statement refuses: `graphspec.operations.pipeline.draw(registry, template, parameters, choose)` draws each entry after the entries before it in the template that name kinds and after the entries it reads, otherwise in the order of the template (`pipeline.ordered`), so that an entry read by an earlier one, as `graphspec.derive` by `graphspec.edges`, is drawn after the entries between them that name kinds; it offers an entry, and each value of its parameters, only where it names no kind an entry drawn before it removed, and removes no kind that an entry after it which the bounds hold present names whatever it is given, nor any kind the target of a kind of task names; what each value names is read from the `kinds` of its operation, one parameter at a time. A `kinds` that reads a number stops naming the operation and the number when it is first read, when a search holding the operation starts or a pipeline holding it is stated: a search cannot try every number, and an operation registered from outside states what it does with kinds by its choices and flags. Bounds that hold an entry present where it can be given nothing stop naming the entry, the kind and the other entry when the search starts. Where the entries before a parameter leave it fewer values than its bounds, TPE knows it under its name followed by the values it offers.

**Derivation**

One engine derives every variant, in segments: a segment is a range of steps of one graph, a whole graph is one segment, and a joined group is a sequence of them on one axis of steps. The views of a bank whose tasks fall in the same splits are ordered by the name of their graph, then their observer and split, and cut into groups of `graphs/join_size` in that order; the place of a graph in the bank never forms a group. Every operation and every representation states the state it carries into the first segment and replaces it in each segment; the engine carries nothing else and knows no operation or representation by name. With one worker the engine runs in the calling process, `graphspec.engine.segments.variant(graphs, beside, registry, steps, spec, groups, seed, limits, partitions, destination)`, which writes the variant of a graph or a joined group; with more, `graphspec.engine.schedule.variants(jobs, registry, steps, spec, groups, seed, workers, limits, partitions)`, each job stating its graphs and their second graphs under `beside`, runs the same phases on worker processes: a phase that carries state runs on the segments of a graph in order, one that carries none on any segment as soon as its input is ready, and parts side by side; the arrays are equal for any number of workers. `graphspec.engine.segments.whole(graph, beside, registry, steps, seed, limits, partitions)` gives the derived graph of one graph as one segment, without representations, `beside` mapping the name of every second graph to that graph.

**Second graphs**

An operation that takes two graphs changes the graph it is given and reads a second graph, named by its entry of the pipeline. The derivation is given, for every second graph its steps name, that graph shown at the steps of each graph it derives, and stops naming both where the names differ or where a second graph shows other steps or other times than the graph beside it. The two graphs never share entity numbers: they meet by keys. An entity of the second graph takes the number of the entity of the graph beside it with the same kind and token, and otherwise a number of its own after every entity of the derivation, so that it is an entity of every segment of that graph; in a joined group that number is one per kind and token across the graphs of the group where the join connects that kind and token, as the physical join of the second graphs unifies them, and one per graph where it does not; the tables of values of the second graphs are placed before those of the graphs of the derivation. A phase of the operation reads, as `carry.second`, the rows of the second graph at the steps of its segment, with their entities numbered as in the derivation and their own tables of values and the rows those tables hold in the variant.

In a bank, the second graph of a view is the graph of the bank of that name shown at the times of the steps of the view, as its observer sees them, with no task, no target and no matured target, and with its steps numbered as its periods; it is built once per view and kept with the views under `beside/`. A second graph that counts time in another unit than the view, or a view whose split states rules of what it may see, stops naming them. The seed a derivation is given is the one every operation receives as `seed` and every representation reads. For the variants of a bank, and the neighbours packing samples from them, it is the `derivation_seed` the statement of the fit or of the search states, required and shared by the trials of a search, which `tuning.bank.bank.Deriver(directory, device, seed, resident)` takes; the library holds no seed of its own.

Every random draw of an operation or a representation is keyed, and `graphspec.infra.hashing.unit(seed, salt, *keys)` gives it as a number in [0, 1): `seed` is the derivation's, `salt` a text naming the draw, and the keys are those of identity: an entity's token, `carry.tokens(entities)` of a segment; for an edge the tokens of its two ends, ordered where it is read symmetrically, and the hash of the name of its relation; the name of the graph, `step_token` of the step; and, for a draw made per step, its time, `step_time`. A draw never reads a position in an array, an entity or step number, a worker, a device or a random generator, so what is drawn does not depend on the order of the rows and entities of the graphs, on the place of a graph among others, on the cuts of segments, on the number of workers or on the device. A draw that chooses among candidates takes the largest weight plus a Gumbel number drawn from each candidate's keys, and where a tie or a cut depends on the order of candidates they are ordered by their keys. The draws shipped, with their salts: sampling of nodes (`graphspec.nodes/sample`, the entity and the graph), dropping of edges (`graphspec.edges/drop`) and random caps (`graphspec.edges/cap_order`), each by the ends, the relation and the graph; rewiring by sdrf (`graphspec.derive/rewiring`, the graph and time of the step, the iteration and the ends of the candidate pair); negative links (`graphspec.negatives/links`, the relation, the graph and time of the step, the node kept and the candidate) and negative sets (`graphspec.negatives/sets`, the graph and time of the step, the set, the role and the member replaced, and the candidate); walks (`graphspec.walks/walks`, `graphspec.walks/period_walks`, the root, the graph and time of its row's step, the sample and lookback, the hop and the candidate event's identity: its kind, the tokens of its ends, its relation or task key, its state and the graph and time of its step); and the unification of joined graphs (`graphs/connect`, the token alone, as it is the same entity in every graph of the group). An operation or representation registered from outside the library draws the same way. Choices that draw nothing break their ties by the same keys, never by a row or an entity number: the cap of edges by the tokens of a pair's ends and its relation's name, the cap of projections by the member's token, `events/cap` by the identity of the events of one participant at one tick, and diffusion computes the scores of a component with its entities in the order of their tokens and cuts equal scores in that order. Merging keeps the last fact about an edge at a step in the order the rule for ties, then the time they hold from, give the facts about one edge. Packing samples the neighbours of an entity before a cutoff the same way (`tuning.batches/neighbors`, the entity's token, the graph and time of the cutoff's step and its half, and the candidate event's identity), from the arrays `entity_token`, `step_token`, `step_time` and `event_identity` of the variant; the candidates of an entity are listed by tick and identity, so the latest ones are cut by identity where ticks tie. Training shuffles the graphs of an epoch and the batches of a chunk the same way (`tuning.batches/graphs`, `tuning.batches/groups`), keyed by the seed of the statement, the epoch and the name of each view, or of the views of a joined group, so the batches of an epoch do not depend on the place of a graph in the bank or on the numbering of entities.

A derivation is a value that other derivations continue from; nothing in it is changed after it is made:

| Call | What it gives |
|---|---|
| `graphspec.engine.segments.Derivation.begun(graphs, beside, registry, steps, spec, groups, seed, limits, partitions, room)` | The derivation at step 0 of graphs in the form the operations work on, holding the state every operation of the steps and every representation of `groups` states for a first segment; `beside` maps the name of every second graph the steps name to that graph shown at the steps of each of the graphs, in their order; `room` is the number of entities the edits of its continuations may add |
| `derivation.continued(high, edit)` | A new derivation holding this one as its history and the segments from its step to the step `high`, with `edit` applied to them; `edit` is none or an edit of the steps of one graph |
| `derivation.forked(high, edits)` | One continuation per edit, sibling forks that share this derivation as their history |
| `derivation.arrays()`, `derivation.derived()` | The arrays of the variant up to its step, and its derived graph |
| `graphspec.engine.edits.sliced(graph, low, high)` | The steps from `low` to `high` of a graph, with its tasks, their references and targets, and the rows of its tables they read |
| `graphspec.engine.edits.Edit(entities, removed, added)`, `edited(graph, edit)` | An edit, and the graph it makes |

A fork is the state of a derivation at a step and an overlay: it holds its parent, whose segments and state it shares and never copies, the segments it derived itself, its own state, and the entities and the rows of values its edits added. The history of every fork taken at one step is derived once. An edit states `entities`, the kinds and tokens of the entities it adds, numbered after those of the graph, and tokens that the graph holds stop it; `removed`, the observations by entity and step and the facts by relation, source, target and step it removes, each of which the graph must hold; and `added`, the observations by entity, step and values and the facts by source, target, relation, step, presence and values it adds, a row of values all missing adding a fact without values. A change of value is the removal of the row and the addition of its new value. Steps are those of the derivation; an added row follows the rows of its step, and an edit naming a step outside the graph stops. The entities an edit adds are numbered after every entity of the derivation, and their rows of values after every row of its graphs; in a joined group they are not unified with the entities of other graphs. A derivation whose edits add more entities than its `room` stops naming both numbers.

**Representations**

A representation is registered in `graphspec.REPRESENTATIONS` under its name as `graphspec.Representation(name, family, carries, horizon, piece, ended, stitched, check, version)`: `family`, one of `structure`, where it gives a model structure the variant does not hold as edges (links, events, derived nodes), `sample`, where it gives a model a choice among what the variant holds (neighbours, walks), and `encode`, where it gives a model values computed from the variant for what it holds; `carries(registry, spec)`, the state it carries into the first segment; `horizon(spec)`, the steps and the groups of periods of history before a segment that it reads, which every segment keeps; `piece(shown, state)`, the arrays of one segment, from the segment `shown` holds and its state, which it replaces; `ended(state)`, none or the rows it adds after the last segment; `stitched(arrays, registry, spec, limits)`, none or the arrays it computes over every segment; and `check(arrays, name, registry, spec)`, which stops where its arrays are wrong. Every variant holds the arrays of observations, facts, tasks and events, which read and carry a state of their own, with `event_identity`, the identity of every event, and `step_time` and `step_token` of every step, and the representations of the groups a model reads: `coupling`, `events`, `neighbors`, `walks` and `mixed`. Their parameters are settings of the pipeline. `coupling`, `events` and `mixed` change structure, `neighbors` and `walks` sample, and none of them encodes; `graphspec.representations.registry.families()` lists every registered representation by family. A representation registered from outside the library is read by no family of models until families state what they read; a model states for each such representation whether its fit derives it beside the groups its family reads, `representations/<name>`, which a search offers like any parameter of the model.

**The key of a variant**

A cached variant is kept under the digest of a record of: `schema`, the fingerprint of the declaration of the bank; `source`, the digest of what its build stored, `stored_sha256` of its manifest; `pipeline`, for every entry its operation, label, second graph, version and parameters; `added`, what every entry adds, by entry; `settings`, those read when deriving; `groups`, the representations derived, those the family of the model reads and those the model states; `splits`, what each split may see; `device`, the device of dense algebra; `seeds`, the seed of derivation the statement states; `versions`, those of the code of views, of derivation and of representations; and `entries`, the registry, name and version of every registered entry the variant reads: those named by the schema (types of column, encoders, encoders of held graphs, sorts of reference, kinds of target, the rule for ties and the rules for edges about edges), the choice of steps, the choice among several facts and the forms of storage the settings state, the rules of what each split may see, the representations of `groups`, the type and encoder of every column an entry adds, and every entry a parameter of an operation chooses by name among the entries of a registry (`graphspec.operations.parameters.registered`, as `negatives/rule` does). Every derivation, in a bank or outside one (`graphspec.engine.segments.whole`, `variant`, `Derivation` and `graphspec.engine.schedule.variants`), runs with the entries it names: those the schema of its registry names (as above), the operations of its pipeline and every entry a parameter of one chooses with `graphspec.operations.parameters.registered`, and, where it is given settings and representations, the forms of storage the settings state and those representations. In a bank these are among the entries the key of the variant holds. They hold in the thread that runs the derivation, and every process it starts receives them with the registered entries. While it runs, a lookup by name of any other entry of a registry whose entries state versions, made by code registered from outside the library, directly or through the library, stops the derivation at once with an error naming that code (its module and function), the registry and the entry, and saying that an entry such code chooses is stated with `graphspec.operations.parameters.registered`, and one the statement or the schema names is read from its place there. This holds for every such code that runs during a derivation: the phases of an operation, the states they carry, their parts, its counts, the functions that state its parameters and their conditions, the tokens of the kinds it adds, and every function of a representation. An operation's `adds` and `kinds` run when a pipeline is stated, keyed or drawn, never during a derivation; the functions that state its parameters run during one only where the registry it derives with has not met them, as in a process the derivation starts. The code of the library is held to its versions instead. Code outside `graphspec/registries.py` reads an entry by `Names.named`, never by the mapping `held`, and `tests/test_standing.py` fails naming any direct read of it in the library or its tests. The key is never completed after the fact. No file is hashed. The folder of the caches of a bank is keyed by the digest of what its build stored and the version of views; the partition index of staged tables by the record of the staging, `stage.json`, which holds the sha256 of every file the staging wrote, computed once when it wrote it, and by its own version, never by the sizes or times of the files (see [the loader](loader.md), Runtime storage). `tests/test_standing.py` holds for every shipped operation, and for the code of derivation, of views, of representations and of the partition index, its version and a digest of its code, and fails naming it when its code changes and its version does not: whoever edits them raises the version. An operation, or any other entry, registered from outside the library is keyed by the version it states; its code is never hashed, so whoever changes it raises its version.

**After an operation**

The schema is checked after an operation for what it states it changes alone, against the registry of the pipeline: a group for every step; the width of node values and the kinds of node; relations the schema states or the pipeline adds, between kinds the relation joins. An operation that changes observations, does not state `references` and leaves a node that a reference names without its observation at the step of the task stops with an error naming the operation and the reference. The node a `link` names is not a reference: an operation may remove it, and the task is then left out (see Kinds of target).

The operation `graphspec.nodes` states `references`. Its parameter `nodes/referenced` says what becomes of a referenced node that it would remove: `keep` leaves its observation at the step of the task, without its edges; `remove` removes it, and is offered where every kind of task states `absent` as `keep`. The node a `link` names is not a referenced node: it is kept or removed as any node its kind holds. `graphspec.neighbourhood` grows its surroundings from the nodes references name, never from the node a `link` names.

**The statement of a fit and the splits**

The statement of a fit, `tuning.statement.Statement`, holds beside its other fields:

| Field | Holds |
|---|---|
| `trained` | The names of the splits trained on, at least one. They are the splits the bank was built for. |
| `scored` | The names of the splits scored on, at least one, none of them trained on. |
| `sees` | For every split trained on or scored on, the list of the rules of what its tasks may see; an empty list states that they see every fact. |
| `loss` | The registered loss the fit trains with (`tuning.LOSSES`). |
| `measures` | The registered measures the fit records, at least one (`tuning.MEASURES`). A loss or a measure that cannot read a kind of target of the bank stops the fit naming both. When a fit or a search starts, before anything is derived or trained, every measure is checked against the targets of the splits scored on as the bank holds them: a series it reads without a point there, or a need of the measure they do not meet (its `refusal`, see tuning/README.md), such as varying targets for `r2` or a pair of tasks to compare for `concordance`, stops it naming the splits, the series or the measure. |

A configuration carries the same three under `splits`, and a fit whose configuration and statement differ in them stops. The rows of a split neither trained on nor scored on enter no fit.

**What a family reads**

A model receives the references of the tasks of a batch as one table of members. Per member it holds `member_row`, the task of the batch it belongs to; `member_name`, the position of its reference among the references of the schema; `member_entity` and `member_index`, its node and the observation of the node at the step of the task; and `member_place`, twice its place plus the end of its edge for a sort that keeps order, and 0 for any other. An edge gives its two nodes, a set of nodes or a subgraph every node it names, a path the nodes of its edges in order; a set edge gives its node where the form of storage makes one and is not observed where it makes none. A member whose node is not observed at the step of the task is left out, so that a kind that states `absent` as `keep` gives the model nothing to read for that node. A reference to the graph is an entry of `whole_row` and `whole_name`. The table is ordered by task, then by the position of the name among the references of the schema, then by place; the order in which a kind states its references reaches no family, and a family reads no reference by its position.

Every family reads of a task:

- every member node, embedded by the family's own machinery, as the table below states. The members of one reference of one task are pooled by their mean, the place of a member of a sort that keeps order added to it first; each reference is projected by a matrix of its own name, and the projections are summed. A reference none of whose members is read gives nothing. What the head reads therefore has one width whatever the number and the sort of the references.
- a reference to the graph, as the pooled state of the graph at the step of the task that the table states.
- the features of the task, which hold the attributes of its graph, and a learned embedding of its kind.
- the series of each point of its target, through a learned embedding, read with the task by the head of the kind of target of the point, one head per kind of target the bank holds; the head of a path also reads the `tau` of the point, and every head the inputs of its kind. No family reads `y` or an outcome of a point: the batch it reads holds neither.
- the node each point of a kind of target that names nodes names, embedded as a member node of the table, with `member_name` one past the references of the schema, and read by the head of the kind of the point alone: `point_node` gives, per point, its row in the table of members, -1 for none. No reference pools such a member, the pair of the task never holds it, the age of its earlier observations enters no gap of its task, and under `affinity` it updates no state of its node.
- the relation of every edge it reads, stated or taken from a column, through a learned embedding of the code of the relation added to what the edge carries: under `snapshot` the message of a `relational` or `relational_attention` spatial layer, under `event_graph` and `memory` the event of the edge, under `projection`, `sequence` and `memory` with attention the interaction partner reached by the edge, under `tree` a node of the tree and under `walks` a hop. Weights held per relation are held per relation stated, per table of relations taken from a column and per derived relation, in each direction. `mixed`, `affinity` and `signal` read no edge, and the other spatial layers of `snapshot` no relation.
- the pair of the task: the two nodes its kind names as `event`, or, where it names none, the two ends of the reference its target is `about` when that reference is of a sort that holds exactly one edge. Features between two nodes are computed for this pair alone, and hold nothing for a task that has none.

| Family | A member node | The graph | The pair |
|---|---|---|---|
| `snapshot` | its state at the frame of the task after the spatial and temporal layers; with a context of earlier periods, gated by its states or features at the ends of those periods, except under `coupling_edges`, which links them itself | the mean state of the frame | nothing |
| `mixed` | a query node holding its features, linked from the derived nodes its node reaches up to the time of the task | the mean features of the nodes shown at the step | nothing |
| `event_graph` | the mean of the events reachable from its node, or, with a context of earlier periods, a node of its own linked from them and from its features at the ends of those periods; beside its features | as `mixed` | nothing |
| `memory` | the state of its node when the task reads, or attention from it over its sampled partners; with a context of earlier periods, gated by its states at their ends | as `mixed` | with `pair_terms`: the product of the two, their common neighbours, listed in the order of their tokens, weighted by frequency, and each scaled by its common-neighbour count |
| `projection` | the Gram matrix of its projections, gated as `memory` with a context, beside its features and the mean of its sampled partners | as `mixed` | the Gram matrix between the projections of the two nodes |
| `sequence` | its sampled partners as tokens, with the ends of earlier periods as tokens under a context | as `mixed` | co-occurrence and overlap of each node's partners with those of the other, their interaction times, and, under `ssm`, selection by the other's summary |
| `tree` | an attention tree rooted at its node, gated by the trees at the ends of earlier periods under a context | as `mixed` | nothing |
| `walks` | walks from its node, and from it inside earlier periods under a context, beside its features | as `mixed` | hitting counts of each node's walks in those of the other |
| `affinity` | a gated state of its node over its counts, by kind, of the tasks seen as events it was first of, with the recent profile of the graph; with a context, gated by those counts at the ends of earlier periods | the counts of every node, with the recent profile | nothing |
| `signal` | its features | the state of the frame of the signals | nothing |

`tuning.families.model_spec.views` offers every family to configurations and searches, `signal` where the tasks carry signals, and `affinity` where a kind of task is seen as an event. No family refuses a sort, a number of references or an absent node, and a search offers every family under every value of `time/context`.

The windows packed into one batch are read apart: no prediction depends on which other windows share its batch, and none reads a fact or an event after the time of its task. A step may hold any number of tasks, and a family reads all of them. Where a family reads a step as a whole, it pools what the tasks of the step give by their mean, as it pools the members of a reference, and a step without a task gives zero: under `snapshot` with `graph_sequence` the frame of a step reads the features of every task seen as an event at the step before it within the window and the states of their two nodes at that step, and feeds back to the first node of each at the frame's own step where that step shows it, so that no frame reads an event of its own step and the first frame of a window reads none; `signal` reads at every step the mean of the signals of the tasks of the step; `affinity` reads as the recent profile of the graph, at every step, the mean over the tasks of the step of the counts of their members plus their kind where it is seen as an event, and its state reads every task of its window.
